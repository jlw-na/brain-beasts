Hatch, train and care for your own creatures

Brain Beasts is a gentle, storybook-styled virtual pet and learning game designed for children aged 3 to 9+. Kids hatch elemental eggs, care for their creatures, play bite-sized educational mini-games in maths, memory, patterns, and spelling, dress up their companions, and challenge friendly storybook bosses.

No ads No in-app purchases Works 100% offline No data collected

Made for young players & peace of mind for parents

  • Ages 3–4, 5–6, 7–8, and 9+: Four tailored age bands inside Parental Controls automatically scale sums, spelling, memory combos, and number patterns.
  • Gentle gameplay: Every try earns rewards, losing a battle simply sends your creature for plasters at the doctor, and creatures pause safely whenever the app is closed.
  • 12 original creatures & 3 evolution paths: Training in Strong, Fast, or Smart visibly shapes how each creature grows from Baby to Kid to Adult.
  • Private by design: No accounts, no tracking, no external links for kids, and all progress stays strictly on your device.
