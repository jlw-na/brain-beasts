Thank you for choosing Brain Beasts!

Welcome, Beast Keeper! Please read this manual with your young player before starting. It explains every screen in the game, all nine training games, battles and bosses, and the settings grown-ups can change. Little ones don't need to read anything to play: every button has a picture, and the game gives a gentle tour the first time.

⚠ Important: please read

Some battles, hatching and the dance party use bright flashes, screen shakes and flashing disco lights. If your child is sensitive to flashing images, or simply prefers things gentle, turn on Calm mode in Settings (see chapter 12). It swaps flashes for a soft glow, stops the shaking and slows the disco floor right down.

Take a break now and then. Your beast will wait patiently: nothing happens to it while the game is closed.

CH.1

Getting Started

Make a player, pick two eggs, and hatch your first beasts.

Controls

Brain Beasts is played entirely with a finger. There are only three moves to learn:

TapPress buttons, answer questions, pat your beast, throw the ball.
DragPick up a plaster at the Doctor and drop it on a sore spot.
TraceJoin the stars, or lead a sleepy sheep along its path, at bedtime.

The big red ✖ in the top-left corner always means "go back" or "stop", wherever you are.

Who's playing?

Each child gets their own player, with their own beasts, coins and clothes. Tap New, type a name (a grown-up can help) and choose one of ten animal pictures. After that, just tap your picture to carry on where you left off.

The gear in the corner opens Settings, and Parents opens the grown-ups' area. Both are explained in chapter 12.

Pick two eggs

Every new player chooses two eggs of different elements. There are six elements: Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Ice and Rock. Each egg holds one of that element's two beasts, chosen by surprise. Once you've hatched your first, its element is ticked off and its picture appears in the egg's corner, so your second beast is always a different kind.

Tap the egg until it wobbles, rattles and bursts open. Then give your baby a name, pick one of seven shades of its element's colour, and choose plain, spots or stripes. Tap Done ✔ and you're ready to play. (You can change the shade, pattern and name later in the Shop.)

Note

The first time a player reaches the home screen, a short tour lights up each meter and the button that fills it. Tap anywhere to move on, or Skip. A grown-up can replay it from Parental controls.

The Home Screen

Everything about your beast, at a glance.

  • 1Player picture. Back to "Who's playing?". Your game is saved automatically.
  • 2House. Visit all your beasts and choose who to look after. It bounces when an egg is waiting there.
  • 3Name, element, stars and growth bar. One star for a Baby, two for a Kid, three for an Adult. The blue bar fills as your beast grows.
  • 4Stats. Strong Fast Smart. Raise them by training.
  • 5Coins. Earned by caring, training and battling. Spend them in the Shop.
  • 6Tummy (apple). Goes down slowly. Fill it with Feed.
  • 7Fun (smiley). Goes down slowly. Fill it with Play.
  • 8Energy (battery). Used by training and battles. Fill it with Sleep.
  • 9Health (heart). Battles can hurt it. Fix it with the Doctor.
  • 10Your beast! Tap it for a pat. Its face shows how it feels, and a thought bubble shows what it needs most.
  • 11Action buttons. Feed, Play, Sleep, Train, Battle, Doctor, Dress up and Shop.
Tip!

Bars turn from green to yellow to red as they run low. If you're not sure what to do next, look at the thought bubble: a plaster means the Doctor, an apple means food, a moon means bedtime, and a ball means playtime.

Note

Meters only go down while the game is open. Close the app for a day or a week and your beast will be exactly as you left it. No sad surprises.

Looking After Your Beast

Feed, play, sleep and the Doctor.

Feed

Tap Feed and three snacks slide up. Tap one and your beast gobbles it down.

SnackWhat it does
🍎 AppleFills the tummy a little
🐟 FishFills the tummy the most, plus a tiny bit of energy
🍰 CakeA small snack, but lots of fun!

Feeding a hungry beast earns a coin. Food is always free.

Play

Tap Play, then tap anywhere in the meadow to throw the ball. Your beast runs after it and leaps to catch it. Five catches fill the circles at the top and make for a lovely game of fetch, worth coins and growth.

You can also just tap your beast any time for a pat. It wiggles happily and grows a tiny bit.

Sleep & dreams

Tap Sleep (it turns into Wake) and the sky goes dark. Every bedtime starts with a short dream game, chosen at random. The better you do, the more sleep stars your beast gets, and the faster it recharges:

The moon bar along the bottom is the timer. In Counting sheep, keep your finger on the road: if you wander off, the road turns purple and the sheep waits. Put your finger back on the sheep to carry on.

Sleep starsRecharge speed
No stars (dream skipped)Normal
★1.3× faster
★★2.2× faster
★★★3.5× faster!
Tip!

After the dream, tap Switch to pop over to the House and play with a different beast. Sleeping beasts keep recharging while you look after someone else, and a little message tells you when they wake up.

The Doctor

Losing a battle can leave your beast feeling sore, and it can't battle again with less than a third of its health. Tap Doctor: glowing sore spots appear on its body. Drag a plaster from the box on the right and drop it on each spot. Three plasters and it's all better!

Health also comes back slowly while your beast sleeps.

Training & the Nine Brain Games

Every stat is trained by a real learning game.

Tap Train and pick a stat. Each stat has three different games, which take turns, so training never gets samey. After a short "3, 2, 1" countdown, the game begins. At the end you earn 1 to 3 stars: more stars means more stat points, plus coins and growth either way.

Training uses a little energy and food, and your beast needs some energy left to start. The coloured bar under the cards shows how many points your beast has trained so far. The striped part is locked until it grows up (see chapter 5).

The games get harder or easier to match each player's age band (3–4, 5–6, 7–8 or 9+), which a grown-up sets in Parental controls. Here's what each one practises.

Strong Memory & comparing numbers

Copy me!
Working memory

Watch your beast punch a combo on the element pads (👀 Watch!), then copy it (👆 Your turn!). Each combo copied charges a glowing power ball, and at the end it blasts the punching bag. Combos get longer as you get them right.

Pick the heavier!
Comparing numbers

Two barbells, two numbers. Tap the bigger one and your beast lifts it. Older players compare sums and times tables, like 9 or 8 + 4.

Match pairs!
Visual memory

Flip two cards at a time to find matching element pairs. Remember where you saw each one! More pairs for older players.

Fast Counting, letters & quick thinking

Dash in order
Counting & the alphabet

Targets appear all over the meadow. Tap them in order (1, 2, 3… or A, B, C…) and your beast zips to each one. Older players count in 2s and 5s or mix 1, A, 2, B. Dots help little ones count.

Catch the match!
Sorting & categories

Only catch the ones that fit the rule at the top: a certain element, letters not numbers, even numbers, vowels, or multiples. Leave the others alone!

Spell it!
Phonics & spelling

A picture appears with empty letter slots. Dash to the letters in the right order to spell it. For 3–4 year olds it's First letter!, just the starting sound.

Smart Sums, patterns & puzzles

Add them up!
Addition & times tables

Your beast chalks a sum on the board, with counting dots to help. Tap the right answer of three. Older players get bigger sums and the 2, 5 and 10 times tables.

What's next?
Patterns & sequences

Work out the pattern (pictures, counting up, counting down, jumps) and pick what comes next.

Find the ?
Missing numbers

Something's missing! Count the stars, take some away, or find the hidden number in a sum. For 3–4 year olds it's How many?, a simple counting game.

Note

There's no "fail" in training. A wrong answer gets a gentle wobble and shows the right one, then the game carries on. Every session earns something.

Starting over with stats

Changed your mind about how you've trained a beast? Once it has trained points, a Reset button appears beside the bar in the Train menu. It's a big change, so a grown-up has to answer a quick sum first (the same one as the Parents button). Then, for 25 coins, its stats go back to where they started. It stays the same age and keeps its look.

Growing Up

Baby → Kid → Adult. How you train shapes how it looks.

Everything your beast does fills its growth bar: eating, playing, sleeping, training and battling. When the bar is full, the screen goes dark, your beast glows and flickers between its old and new shapes, and then, flash!, it grows up. A card shows its stats and gives you a coin bonus.

StageStarsMax training points
Baby★45
Kid★★90
Adult★★★150

The points are shared between all three stats, so you can make an all-rounder or put everything into one stat for a super-strong, super-fast or super-smart beast.

Training paths

Each time your beast grows, whatever it trained most changes how it looks, and the changes build up at Adult:

  • Strong beasts get bigger and bolder, with armour, horns, tusks or mighty fists, and a power ring at their feet.
  • Fast beasts get sleek and swept-back, with wings, fins, wind swooshes and speedy streaks.
  • Smart beasts get a glowing forehead gem, twinkly eyes, and magical crowns, halos and sparkles.

Mix two kinds of training and you'll get a mix of both looks. Every beast has its own versions, so it's worth trying them all!

CH.6

Battles

Pick a challenger and watch your training pay off.

Choosing an opponent

  • Easy (green), Medium (yellow) and Hard (red). Harder opponents are stronger but pay more coins.
  • The badge on the top left shows the opponent's element.
  • A green up arrow on the top right means your element beats theirs. A red down arrow means theirs beats yours.
  • The same three opponents stay until you beat one. Lose to a tough one? Train up and try again!
  • The dice swap all three for new ones for 5 coins. Tap once (it turns into a tick) and again to pay, so a slip of the finger costs nothing.

The fight

Battles play out by themselves. Sit back and cheer! Each beast has a health bar and, under it, a thin blue turn gauge. Every stat shows up in its own way:

  • Strong hits harder and shrugs off damage with a shield glint.
  • Fast fills the turn gauge sooner for Quick! double turns, and sometimes Dodges!
  • Smart lands sparkly Super! hits that do double damage.

The fast-forward button (top right) doubles the speed.

Win or lose

Win and you get coins and growth, and three fresh opponents. Sometimes a win even drops an egg of a beast you don't have yet (more often on Hard). Hatch it straight away or keep it in the House for later.

Lose and you still get a couple of coins. Your beast will be a bit sore, so pay a visit to the Doctor.

CH.7

Boss Battles

Twelve royal beasts. Can you beat them all?

The purple card under the three wild beasts is the Boss: a crowned king, queen, giant or mega version of one of the twelve beasts, fought in a moonlit castle courtyard. Bosses are tough, with extra health and lots of training.

  • Only Adults can take on a boss. Until then the card shows a lock.
  • The same boss waits until it's beaten. You can pay 20 coins (the dice) to meet a different one.
  • The number beside "Boss" counts how many of the 12 bosses you've beaten.
  • Boss battles use more energy and food than a normal battle.

The prize

Beat a boss and your beast becomes a Champion. It wins the golden crown (only champions may wear it!), a pile of coins and a golden egg. Each beast can only become champion once, so to beat all twelve bosses you'll need to raise lots of Adults.

Hatch the golden egg to choose an element. The egg will hatch a beast of that element that you don't have yet. The little pictures in each egg's corner show the beasts you already own of that element (and any eggs still waiting in the House). Once you have both beasts of an element, its egg is greyed out.

Secret

A beast with every single training point filled (all 150 as an Adult) is guaranteed to beat any boss, however its points are shared out. Lose to a boss? Lose a bit of energy, earn a few coins, and keep training!

Tip!

Check the boss's element badge. Bring an Adult whose element beats it (look for the green arrow) and the fight gets much easier.

The Shop & Dress Up

Hats, glasses, neckwear, scenes and paint.

Spend your coins in the Shop. The tabs along the top are hats, glasses, neckwear, backgrounds and paint. Tap an item to try it on your beast in the preview, then tap the price to buy it. A green tick means you already own it.

  • Clothes and backgrounds belong to the player, so every one of your beasts can wear them.
  • Each beast keeps its own outfit. Use Dress up to change it any time for free.
  • The paint tab changes your beast's shade or pattern for a few coins, and its name for free.

Special items

Set Mastery items (marked with a sparkle and a lock) unlock once you've bought every regular item in that tab: the Galaxy Crown, Rainbow Shades, Royal Cape and the Rainbow Castle background.

Achievement items can't be bought at all. They're earned!

The House & Your Collection

Where all your beasts live, and where the secrets are kept.

Tap the house button on the home screen to visit. Your beasts wander about, hop, and have little thoughts. Sleeping beasts nap upstairs and show how charged up they are.

  • Choose who to look after. Tap any beast (or its slot on the shelf) to see its card and a short story about it, then tap Play!. Beasts you're not looking after are paused, just as if the game were closed.
  • The collection shelf along the bottom has a slot for all twelve kinds of beast. Ones you haven't found yet are shadows with a question mark.
  • Eggs waiting (from bosses or battle drops) sit in the top corner. Tap one to hatch it.
  • The roof holds a locked secret egg, with a count of how many kinds of beast you've raised to Adult.

Tap Bosses in the top corner to visit the Boss House, and My House to come home.

Secret

Raise all twelve kinds of beast to Adult and the egg on the roof unlocks. Tap it ten times in the dark... and find out what's inside! (Hint: bring your dancing shoes.) Once it's hatched, tap it on the roof any time to start the fun again.

Hints & Tips

Secrets from the Beast Keepers' Club.

Caring

  • A happy beast is a healthy beast. Keep the tummy and fun bars out of the red and it'll smile at you.
  • Cake is the quickest way to cheer up a bored beast when you don't have time for fetch.
  • Go for three sleep stars at bedtime. A ★★★ sleep recharges more than three times as fast.
  • Got lots of beasts? Put one to bed, then Switch and train another while the first one recharges.

Training

  • Train your beast's favourite stat to get the most out of every point in battle.
  • Decide what kind of beast you want before it grows up. Its look changes with whatever it trained most at each stage.
  • All the stats are worth the same overall. A strong, fast or smart beast can win just as often; they simply fight differently.
  • Training points are capped until the next growth stage, so once the bar is full, battle and care to grow up faster.

Battling

  • Look for the green arrow before you pick a fight. Element advantage really helps.
  • Hard battles pay the most and are the likeliest to drop an egg, but make sure your beast is ready.
  • Can't beat a tough opponent? It'll still be there after some training. No need to spend coins on the dice.
  • For bosses, keep a few Adults in different elements so you always have one with the advantage.

Collecting

  • Golden eggs from bosses let you choose which beast to hatch. Use them on the beasts you're missing.
  • Buy every hat (or every pair of glasses, every neckwear, every background) to unlock that tab's Set Mastery item.
  • Train each stat ten times for three free achievement items!

For Grown-ups

Settings, parental controls and saving.

Settings

Tap the gear on the "Who's playing?" screen. Settings apply to the whole device, for every player.

  • Sounds: sound effects on or off.
  • Music: all the music (home, bedtime, battles, boss battles and the dance party) on or off.
  • Volume: quiet, medium or loud.
  • Calm mode: no white flashes, screen shakes or flashing disco lights. Recommended for children who are sensitive to flashing images.

Parental controls

Tap Parents on the "Who's playing?" screen (or in Settings) and answer a times-table question to get in. For each player you can:

  • Set the age band (3–4, 5–6, 7–8 or 9+). This changes the training games' difficulty straight away.
  • Name & picture: change the player's name or animal picture.
  • Reset creatures: start again with two new eggs, keeping all coins, clothes and backgrounds.
  • Show tour again: replay the home screen tour next time they play.
  • Remove player: deletes the player from this device. It takes three steps, including typing the player's name, so it can't happen by accident.
  • Save backup: saves every player to one file (on a Fire tablet, in Documents › Brain Beasts). Keep a copy somewhere safe, such as email or cloud storage.
  • Load backup: brings players back from a backup file, for example after reinstalling the app or on a new tablet.
  • Game manual (under the players): opens this manual in the web browser.
  • Leave a review (in the tablet app): opens the game's page in the app store, where you can rate it. Reviews help other families find Brain Beasts.
  • About & credits: who made the game, the music credits, and the open-source software inside the tablet app, with its licences (no internet needed).
  • At the bottom: the game's version, and the last problem the game hit, if any, with a button to copy it into an email to us.

Saving

The game saves automatically as you play, on this device only. There are no accounts, no logins and no internet connection needed, and nothing is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes its saves, so use Save backup in Parental controls now and then. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Note

Brain Beasts has no ads, no links out of the game (apart from this manual and the store page for leaving a review, both behind the grown-ups' gate), no chat and no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing.
