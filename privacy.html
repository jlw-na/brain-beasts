Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version: Brain Beasts does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. Everything runs offline on your device.

1. Information We Collect

None. Brain Beasts does not collect personal information (such as names, email addresses, location data, photos, audio, or device identifiers) and does not transmit any data over the internet.

  • No accounts or logins are required to play.
  • No advertising or third-party ad networks are included.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No in-app purchases or external web links are accessible to children.

2. On-Device Game Saves

Player profiles (such as the first name or nickname typed in by a child, chosen avatar, creatures, outfits, and progress) are stored solely on your device in local app storage. This data never leaves your device unless you enable your device's own system backup (such as Apple iCloud Backup or Amazon Device Backup), which is controlled by your device settings and governed by Apple's or Amazon's privacy policies.

You can delete any player profile or reset creatures at any time inside the app's Parental Controls menu (protected by a multiplication gate), or delete all saved data by uninstalling the app.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Australian Privacy Principles)

Brain Beasts is designed for children aged 3 to 9+ and complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Amazon Appstore Child-Directed App Policy, the Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines, and the Australian Privacy Principles. Because the app collects no personal information and contains no third-party tracking or advertising, no child data is ever collected or shared.

4. Support Emails

If a parent or guardian contacts us directly by email for support, we only use your email address and message to reply to your enquiry. We do not share your email address with third parties or add you to any mailing list.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please email us at brainbeasts.support@gmail.com.
