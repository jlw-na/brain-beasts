Support & Parent Guide

Need help with Brain Beasts, spotted a bug, or have a suggestion? Send us an email and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

For a bug, these details help us track it down quickly:

  • Your tablet, for example Fire HD 10 or iPad (9th generation)
  • Where you got the app: Amazon Appstore or Apple App Store
  • Which screen or minigame you were on
  • What happened just before the problem, and what you expected instead
  • A screenshot, if you can take one

The email button fills in these headings for you. Please leave out your child's full name and other personal details, as we don't need them to help.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change the difficulty for my child's age?

On the Who's playing? screen, tap Parents and solve the multiplication question. Inside Parental Controls you can set each player's age band separately: 3–4, 5–6, 7–8, or 9+. This adjusts the sums, spelling words, number patterns, and memory games for that child.

Do creatures get hungry or sick while the app is closed?

No. All meters pause automatically whenever the app is closed or the tablet is asleep. Kids can put the tablet down for days and come back to find their creatures just as they left them.

How do we start fresh without losing bought clothes and backgrounds?

Open Parents on the player select screen and tap Reset creatures next to the player's name. This lets them pick two fresh starter eggs while keeping all coins, outfits, and backgrounds they have already bought or unlocked.

There's no sound — how do I fix it?

Check that your tablet's volume is turned up and that the silent / mute switch is off. On some tablets, sound activates right after your first tap inside the app.

Where are saved games stored?

All player profiles are saved directly on your tablet with no account required. Because the app works completely offline, uninstalling the app will remove local saves unless your tablet's system backup is enabled.

When reporting a bug, what helps most?

Your tablet model, where you got the app, the screen you were on, and what happened just before the issue. The checklist at the top of this page has the full list, and the email button fills in the headings for you. Screenshots are always welcome!
